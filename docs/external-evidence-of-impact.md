# External Evidence Of Impact

This page preserves public professional and teaching context. It does not claim employer-specific results or validate the outcomes of the operating models in this repository.

## Weight the Sources

Not all public sources carry the same weight.

- professional-body sources can establish recognition within their stated scope
- interviews and teaching materials show the ideas discussed, not realized business outcomes
- self-authored bios can be useful context, but they should not be treated as independent evidence

## Strongest External Signals

### 1. Official external professional recognition tied to senior operating scope

Source:

- [Institute of Analytics Fellow profile](https://ioaglobal.org/ioa-fellows/paras-doshi/)

What it supports:

- external recognition from a professional body

The profile provides historical professional context. It is not verification of a current role or of this repository's operating model.

Caveat:

This is an official professional-body profile, which is strong for recognition and scope, but not the same as a detailed business case study.

## Evidence boundary

These sources support public professional recognition, writing, and teaching context. They are not audited business outcomes, verification of a current role, or proof that a particular operating model caused a result. The reusable guides should be assessed on their reasoning, methods, and evidence from your own pilot.
