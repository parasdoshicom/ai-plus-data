# Public Work

This folder is the public record of Paras Doshi's writing, talks, workshops, and field notes on AI, data leadership, and data careers.

Start with the [`2026 year-to-date archive`](./2026.md).

The same inventory is available as a machine-readable [`catalog.csv`](./catalog.csv). Run `python3 scripts/validate_publications.py` from the repo root to check required fields, dates, public URLs, duplicates, counts, and catalog-to-archive coverage.

## What Belongs Here

- work that is already public and can be verified at a public URL
- original LinkedIn posts, newsletters, articles, talks, workshops, and recordings
- the exact public title or opening line, publication date, channel, and link
- a short repo connection when the public work has a deeper playbook, toolkit, or example here

## What Does Not Belong Here

- drafts, scheduled posts, or ideas that have not shipped
- private Telegram, Stanley, Slack, email, or direct-message history
- reposts without original commentary
- personal posts unrelated to AI, data leadership, or data careers
- private company detail, customer context, internal metrics, or operating receipts

## Source And Verification Rules

The public platform is the source of truth. Stanley can help locate or reconcile content, but a bot message or scheduled-content view is not proof that something was published.

Each entry should carry one of two labels:

- **Verified public**: the public URL resolved and the item was visibly published.
- **Public URL recorded**: the URL was captured from a public feed or profile, but the destination was not re-opened during the latest refresh.

The 2026 archive was rebuilt from Paras's public LinkedIn activity and the Insight Extractor RSS feed. Private chat content was not copied into this repo.

## Refresh Cadence

Update the annual archive after a publication batch and at least once a month:

1. read the current LinkedIn activity and Insight Extractor feed
2. reconcile against Stanley as a secondary completeness check
3. add only published, original, in-scope items
4. preserve the exact public title or opening line
5. link a deeper repo artifact when one exists
6. run the publication-safety checklist and link checks
7. update the verified-through date and counts

The goal is a useful body of work, not a vanity counter. Channel activity, reused ideas, and evidence strength stay explicit.
