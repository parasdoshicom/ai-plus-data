# World-Class AI + Data Operator

An open playbook for leading AI and data work from a business decision to a reliable production workflow. Built for heads of data, AI leaders, managers, and practitioners who need results people can trust.

Use the operating models, templates, and synthetic examples to choose useful work, establish decision rights, validate outcomes, and improve the next run. This repository remains the home of AI+Data; its existing paths stay available.

## Use it with your agent

Clone or open this repository, then give your file-capable agent a bounded request:

```text
Read docs/ai-leadership-operating-guide.md and
toolkits/workflow-investment-brief.md.
Help me choose one recurring workflow to pilot.
Ask for the decision, available evidence, permissions, and constraints.
Use placeholders for unknowns. Do not invent current company facts.
Draft the investment brief in my private workspace, with a baseline,
review gate, stop rule, and the next action I can take this week.
```

Keep real company context and completed briefs in your own private workspace. These files guide the work; they do not connect to business systems or grant authority to act.

## The operating model

1. **Choose the decision.** Start with a recurring business problem and the person who must act on the answer.
2. **Define trusted context.** Establish metric definitions, approved sources, permissions, freshness, and caveats.
3. **Prove one workflow.** Run the real path, compare against a baseline, and inspect errors and review effort.
4. **Earn wider use.** A working solo run comes before a team handoff; a working team pilot comes before shared infrastructure.
5. **Make corrections durable.** Review the miss, change the relevant definition or rule, and rerun a regression case.

These are reusable operating practices, not a claim that one architecture fits every organization. Examples labeled synthetic are teaching material, not company results.
